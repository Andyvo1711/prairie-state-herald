Illinois' biggest beauty retailer is growing faster than it expected. Ulta Beauty, headquartered in Bolingbrook, reported second-quarter fiscal 2026 results on August 27, 2026, and raised its full-year guidance, according to the company's filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission.

## The quarter in numbers

For the 13 weeks ended August 1, 2026, net sales rose 8.9 percent to $3,035.7 million, up from $2,788.5 million a year earlier. Comparable sales increased 3.8 percent, compared with 6.7 percent in the prior-year quarter.

Operating income climbed 10.1 percent to $379.6 million, a 12.5 percent operating margin. Diluted earnings per share were $6.55, up 13.3 percent from $5.78. Gross margin slipped slightly to 39.1 percent from 39.2 percent, a change the filing attributes partly to the business mix from the Space NK acquisition.

## Growth drivers

Ulta said its results were helped by the Space NK acquisition, new stores and new brand launches. Space NK is a luxury beauty retailer in the United Kingdom and Ireland. The company also continues to execute what it calls its "Ulta Beauty Unleashed" strategy.

Chief Executive Kecia Steelman said the company was "translating our Ulta Beauty Unleashed strategy into tangible benefits," and tied the performance to innovation, value, experiences and convenience.

## A higher forecast

Ulta lifted its fiscal 2026 outlook across the board. Net sales growth is now expected at 6.7 percent to 7.2 percent, up from 6 to 7 percent. Comparable sales growth is projected at 3.2 to 3.7 percent, and diluted earnings per share at $28.70 to $29.00, compared with a prior range of $28.36 to $28.80. Capital spending guidance of $400 million to $450 million was unchanged.

## Stores and buybacks

The chain ended the quarter with 1,622 stores, including 1,534 in the United States and 88 internationally. During the quarter it opened 15, closed one, relocated three and remodeled seven.

Ulta also increased its fiscal 2026 share repurchase plan to $1.8 billion from $1.5 billion, and expects to use the remaining $1.0 billion of its current authorization by year-end.

## Balance sheet snapshot

Inventories stood at $2.4 billion, essentially flat from last year, while cash and equivalents fell to $158.5 million from $242.7 million a year earlier, the filing shows.
