Cold plunge and contrast therapy studios have multiplied across Naperville over the past year, part of a broader wave of recovery-focused wellness businesses capitalizing on growing mainstream interest in cold exposure therapy that was once mostly confined to professional athletes.

Several new studios have opened offering appointment-based access to cold plunge pools alongside infrared saunas, allowing clients to alternate between cold and heat exposure in structured sessions typically lasting 45 minutes to an hour. Studio owners say demand has grown steadily as social media content about cold exposure's purported benefits for inflammation and mental clarity has reached a wider general audience.

"A few years ago, this was something you'd only see in a professional sports training facility," said the owner of a Naperville contrast therapy studio that opened last year. "Now I have clients coming in on their lunch break from office jobs who just want to feel more alert for the rest of their day."

Membership pricing at the new studios generally runs higher than a typical gym membership, reflecting the specialized equipment and maintenance costs associated with keeping cold plunge pools consistently chilled to target temperatures. Several studio owners said they have found strong demand among clients in their thirties and forties seeking recovery-focused wellness options that feel more targeted than a general fitness membership.

Medical experts have cautioned that scientific evidence for many claimed benefits of cold exposure therapy remains limited compared with the enthusiasm surrounding the trend, though most agree the practice carries relatively low risk for healthy adults when done in a supervised setting. Studio owners in Naperville said they screen new clients for cardiovascular conditions before permitting cold plunge access.
