CME Group, the Chicago-based derivatives exchange operator, reported second-quarter 2026 results on July 22, saying the first half of the year was the strongest in the company's history.

The company reported revenue of $1.7 billion and operating income of $1.1 billion for the quarter. Net income was $1.0 billion, or $2.88 per diluted share. On an adjusted basis, net income was $1.1 billion and earnings were $2.99 per share.

## Volume and fees

Average daily volume in the quarter reached 29.8 million contracts, the third-highest quarterly figure on record, including non-U.S. average daily volume of 9.1 million contracts. Clearing and transaction fees revenue totaled $1.4 billion, with an average rate per contract of $0.678.

Market data revenue rose 20 percent to a record $238 million.

## What management said

Chairman and Chief Executive Terry Duffy said record first-half results across revenue, adjusted operating income, adjusted net income and adjusted earnings per share were powered by record trading in the first quarter and the second-highest second-quarter volumes in company history.

Duffy said CME provided more than $95 billion in daily margin efficiencies during the quarter, a new high. He also pointed to new products and tools, including single-stock futures, 1-ounce gold contracts available around the clock, U.S. Treasury clearing and compute futures.

## Balance sheet

As of June 30, the company held $2.3 billion in cash and $3.4 billion of debt. It paid about $468 million in dividends during the quarter and repurchased $695 million of its common shares.

CME operates the Chicago Mercantile Exchange, the Chicago Board of Trade, NYMEX and COMEX, and its results are closely watched as a gauge of activity in the Chicago financial sector.
