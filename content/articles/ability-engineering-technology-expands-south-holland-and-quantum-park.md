A south suburban precision manufacturer is plugging into Illinois' quantum push. Gov. JB Pritzker and the Illinois Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity announced on July 23 that Ability Engineering Technology (AET) will expand through a two-location investment in South Holland and at the Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park (IQMP) in Chicago.

## Two sites

AET has been headquartered in South Holland since 1947. It designs and builds engineered-to-order process equipment for cryogenic, vacuum and other industrial applications, according to the state.

The company will modernize its existing 35,000-square-foot South Holland plant and open a satellite office at the IQMP. That office will focus on rapid prototyping, small-scale fabrication, equipment modification and workforce training for park tenants and affiliated research institutions.

AET is the second announced tenant of the IQMP FaQtory, a building meant to house suppliers that make equipment and components for companies at the park. The FaQtory is partly funded by the federal Tech Hubs competition and partly by state quantum funds. Zero Point Cryogenics has also been announced as a tenant.

## What the company says

"For nearly 80 years, Ability Engineering Technology has built a global reputation for precision manufacturing and engineering excellence in South Holland," AET President Eugene Botsoe said in the release. He said the expansion builds on that legacy while advancing the state's ambition in quantum technology.

## The numbers

Through an agreement under the state's EDGE incentive program, AET has committed to investing at least $1.8 million, creating at least five new full-time jobs and retaining 23 positions, according to the state's release.

DCEO Director Kristin Richards said the expansion shows the momentum at the park, where the state is building a supplier network for quantum companies. Cook County Board President Toni Preckwinkle said the county is proud to support investments that strengthen the regional economy and create jobs.
