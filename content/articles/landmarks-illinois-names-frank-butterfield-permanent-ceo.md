Landmarks Illinois has named Frank Butterfield its permanent president and chief executive officer, the organization announced October 22, according to Whig.

Butterfield had served as interim president and CEO since July. He previously was the group's chief operating officer.

## A long tenure

Butterfield joined Landmarks Illinois in 2013 as director of its Springfield office, where he expanded advocacy and public outreach. He became chief operating officer in 2021, overseeing daily operations and finances.

In that role he helped develop and carry out the organization's latest five-year strategic plan, known as the Transformational Plan. He also led Landmarks Illinois' acquisition of Illinois Main Street, a network focused on commercial corridors, downtown districts and small business development.

Before Landmarks Illinois, he directed the Wisconsin Field Office of the National Trust for Historic Preservation and held roles at the Roger Brown Study Collection and DePaul University. He holds a chemistry degree from Boston College and a master's in historic preservation from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago.

## What the board says

Board chair Ron Clewer called Butterfield "the ideal leader to guide Landmarks Illinois into its next chapter."

Butterfield said historic preservation has immense potential to strengthen and uplift Illinois communities. Under his leadership, the organization's priorities include community and economic development, housing access and climate change mitigation.

## About the organization

Founded in 1971, Landmarks Illinois describes itself as the only statewide historic preservation nonprofit in Illinois. It is a membership-based group.
