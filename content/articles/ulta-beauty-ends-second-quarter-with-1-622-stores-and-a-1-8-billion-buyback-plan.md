The headline from Ulta Beauty's second-quarter report was a lift in guidance, but the fine print offers a clearer view of how the Bolingbrook company is running its business. The figures come from the earnings release the company furnished to the Securities and Exchange Commission on August 27, 2026.

## Where the stores stand

Ulta ended the quarter, which closed August 1, with 1,622 stores: 1,534 in the United States and 88 internationally. In the quarter it opened 15 locations, closed one, relocated three and remodeled seven. The split reflects both expansion and upkeep of its existing footprint.

The international count includes the stores added through Space NK, a luxury beauty retailer in the U.K. and Ireland that Ulta acquired. The filing says the acquisition contributed to sales growth, to higher selling and administrative expenses and to a slight pressure on gross margin, which was 39.1 percent versus 39.2 percent a year earlier.

## Returning cash to shareholders

The company raised its fiscal 2026 share repurchase plan to $1.8 billion from $1.5 billion. It expects to use the remaining $1.0 billion of its current authorization by the end of the fiscal year.

At the same time, cash and cash equivalents were $158.5 million at quarter end, compared with $242.7 million a year earlier, with another $55.0 million in short-term investments.

## Inventory discipline

Net merchandise inventories were $2,406.7 million, practically unchanged from $2,407.1 million a year ago. The filing says inventory supported new brand launches and new stores, even as sales grew 8.9 percent.

## A steady plan

Chief Executive Kecia Steelman attributed growth to innovation, value, experiences and convenience under the company's "Ulta Beauty Unleashed" strategy. Ulta did not change its planned capital spending of $400 million to $450 million for the year.

For Illinois, where the company is headquartered, the report is a reminder that one of the state's best-known beauty brands is still expanding. The company's loyalty program is referenced in the filing, but it did not disclose a member count in the document.
