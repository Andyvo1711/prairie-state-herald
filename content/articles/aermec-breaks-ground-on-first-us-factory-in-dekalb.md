Italian heating and cooling manufacturer Aermec broke ground in DeKalb on October 8 for what will be its first manufacturing site in the United States, the Illinois Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity (DCEO) announced.

The company is investing $15 million in a facility of more than 186,000 square feet that is expected to create 30 new full-time jobs in the DeKalb region, according to the state.

## Chosen after a multistate search

Aermec North America makes heating, ventilation and air conditioning equipment. The new plant will support production of climate control and energy management system products, DCEO said.

Luca Martini, managing director of Aermec North America, said the groundbreaking marks the beginning of a new chapter for the company in North America. He said Aermec has served U.S. and Canadian customers for more than 20 years and that the state's support and collaboration drove the decision to invest in Illinois.

Melissa Amedeo, executive director of the DeKalb County Economic Development Corporation, said the company chose DeKalb after a competitive search that included sites across multiple states.

## State incentives

The project is backed by an Economic Development for a Growing Economy (EDGE) agreement with the state. DCEO said companies in the EDGE program have committed to nearly 6,000 new jobs and more than $2.6 billion in investment so far in 2026.

"Illinois is committed to fostering long-term economic growth by supporting industries and companies, like Aermec," DCEO Director Kristin Richards said in the release, pointing to the EDGE program as a tool for helping companies invest.

The state also expects construction activity and business for local suppliers and service providers from the project.

## Part of a manufacturing push

The governor's office placed Aermec among a growing list of manufacturers expanding in Illinois this year, including Voortman Steel Machinery, Ability Engineering Technology, Combe and Hyundai Translead. The announcement came during October, which is National Manufacturing Month.
