For skin care professionals, Illinois' new apprenticeship law may change how careers begin. A guide distributed July 28, 2026, by business directory MyTSV explains how House Bill 3460 applies to estheticians.

## A path without tuition first

Under the law, aspiring estheticians can train hands-on under a licensed professional while earning wages, rather than paying for beauty school up front. The law was signed by Gov. J.B. Pritzker on June 26, 2026, and takes effect January 1, 2027, the guide says.

MyTSV compares this with the classroom route of about 750 hours at an approved school, which it says typically costs $10,000 to $20,000 in tuition. Apprentices would sit for the same state licensing exam and meet the same competency standards as school graduates, and licensing fees run close to $200 on either path, according to the guide.

## What was undecided

When the guide was published, the required apprenticeship hours for estheticians had not been set because the Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation was still working on program rules. MyTSV speculated that an esthetics apprenticeship could take close to twice as long as school, but stressed that this is its own estimate and not a rule. Later reporting from a Des Plaines school listed 1,500 practical hours for estheticians, based on its reading of the statute.

## What salons would need

The guide says sponsoring salons must be licensed, hold a Department of Labor-approved apprenticeship agreement, document supervised hours and keep up sanitation and scope-of-practice compliance for each apprentice. It suggests owners assess which licensed staff can teach, and line up a school or partner for theory such as anatomy, chemistry and infection control.

## Legislative background

The bill passed the House Health Care Licenses Committee unanimously in March 2026 and cleared the General Assembly in May, the guide says. It credits Rep. Michael Crawford as sponsor and cites the Institute for Justice in saying about 40 other states offer some beauty or barbering apprenticeship route.

Because the guide is a company press release, readers should confirm details with the General Assembly and state regulators.
